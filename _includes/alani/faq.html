What are your operating hours?

We’re open Wednesday–Friday 10 AM – 4 PM and weekends & public holidays 9 AM – 5 PM. Last order is 2:30 PM.

What types of bread & pastries do you offer?

We focus on Greek and Mediterranean flavours inspired by Chef Alberto’s grandmothers — a “Nomadic Dough” full of nostalgia.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes, chefs occasionally prepare GF & VEGAN options. Please, keep in mind that we are not celiac friendly.

Do you provide pick-up and delivery?

We're working on it! Keep an eye on our updates — we want to make it easy for you to enjoy Alani at home.

Are walk-ins allowed, or is it reservation-only?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For a smoother experience and shorter waiting time, we recommend making a reservation.

Do you accept private bookings (12+ pax)?

Yes. For events of 12 guests or more, email us at reservations@thepotatoheroes.com to plan it.

Is the outdoor space covered?

Yes. Our outdoor area is partially covered and equipped with fans, so you can enjoy the breeze while staying comfortable.

Are pets allowed?

Absolutely! We’re pet-friendly — bring your furry friends along for a relaxed day out.

Where is Alani located?

4th floor of KADA building in the CBD. Nearest MRT: Maxwell, Tanjong Pagar, and Chinatown.
