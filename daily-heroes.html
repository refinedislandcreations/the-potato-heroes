THE DAILY HEROES

We Don't Just Make Food

We make people happy.

Chef Alberto

aka Tettos

Creator & Founder

Behind all three of our exciting projects is a chef who never sleeps and is always, at heart, an explorer. With over 20 years of experience in the food and beverage industry across the globe, he embodies flexibility and dynamism while being the mastermind behind all the fun moments we share.

His love and passion for food shine through every curated concept. A deep-rooted nomad, he skillfully blends modern and traditional recipes and ingredients to create truly unique dining experiences.

His motto? "Food is togetherness."

Geraldine

aka Gigi

Co-Founder & Voice of Our Projects

While our chef expresses his passion through food, Gigi brings it to life through words. As the co-founder, she's the heartbeat of our endeavors. Known for her incredible multitasking skills and problem-solving prowess, she manages both the back of house (BOH) and front of house (FOH) operations.

You might even catch her strolling around with a glass of wine, ensuring that the good vibes are flowing just right!

Jeanette

aka JJ

The Hospitality Queen

What JJ is most known for? Her unmatched hospitality! With her biggest and warmest smile, she greets every guest like a friend, genuinely caring about their dining experience with us. Her superpower? A laughter that fills the room!

With nearly three years on the team, her vibrant personality is a beacon of cheer that brightens our space.

Mr. Gan

Operations Manager

The newest addition to our team, Mr. Gan brings decades of experience in the food and beverage industry from Singapore and Malaysia. As the operations manager, he coordinates all our projects to ensure smooth operations and inspires the team to consistently deliver exceptional results for our guests.

Together, we create an atmosphere of creativity, joy, and culinary excellence.
Come and experience the magic we bring to your table!
